About

World Concept

Bring the digital world to life with everyday IT & cybersecurity problems turned into character-driven battles. On one side defenders: support teams & security working to keep systems stable. On the other the attackers: malware & system failures trying to break through.

Gameplay Logic

Each fighter is built around how their real-world counterpart behaves. Every move, HUD element, warning banner, and attack effect is designed to show players how systems fail, how attackers strike, and how defenders respond.

Why it exists

Built For Memory

System Strike makes technical concepts easier to remember by turning them into characters, attacks, counters, failures, and recoveries.

Visual Learning

Cybersecurity terms become memorable because players see them act, fail, defend, and recover inside the game world.

Arcade Logic

Moves are not random effects. They map to real technology behaviors, from phishing lures to endpoint isolation and recovery states.

Expandable IP

The universe can grow into characters, stages, labs, lessons, animations, collectibles, and a full game design framework.

Match systems

Health Meter & Energy

System Integrity

As fighters take damage, their System Integrity degrades. When it reaches zero, the character enters SYSTEM FAILURE.

Data

Data powers a fighter's strongest abilities. Advanced attacks consume more, but deal heavier damage to the opponent's System Integrity.

Connection State

Connection State gives fighters temporary defensive advantages, helping protect their System Integrity from incoming damage.

Together, System Integrity, Data, and Connection State form the core of System Strike’s gameplay. Each fighter uses these resources differently, creating unique mechanics, matchups, and move combinations based on their role in the digital world.
